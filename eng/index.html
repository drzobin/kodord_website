Penetration testing, red teaming and security audits

Kodord AB offers penetration testing, red teaming, and security audits of entire IT environments, information systems, parts of information systems, and/or specific applications including web applications. We base our work on the client's assignment description and its defining adversarial threats during execution. Upon completion, the client receives a report presenting methodologies, findings and vulnerabilities with severity assessments alongside proposed mitigating measures. We can also support the client with the implementation and prioritization of these mitigating measures.

DDMail

DDMail is an email system designed with security as its primary focus. It has been developed to withstand threats from advanced adversarial actors. DDMail follows the design principles: "Less code, less attack surface", "Least privilege", "Default deny" and "Assume breach". DDMail employs various forms of separation and encryption to protect users' data. It is available as a SaaS solution or can be run On-Prem.

DDMail SaaS

Kodord AB provides DDMail as a SaaS service on the internet, hosted on servers located in Sweden. You can learn more about the service at www.ddmail.se.

DDMail On-Prem

Kodord AB offers support, training, installation assistance, and consulting services for On-Prem installations of DDMail.
